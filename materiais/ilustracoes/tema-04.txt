Minimalist flat geometric illustration in the style of Swiss/Bauhaus diagrams (Josef Müller-Brockmann, Bruno Munari). Square 1024x1024. Pure white background (#FFFFFF), no texture, no shadow, no gradient, no grain. STRICT DUOTONE: exactly two colors of paint — dark ink #231f20 and red #db1e2f — nothing else, no gray, no third color. Mostly outlines; solid fills only for a few small shapes. The figure occupies about 65% of the square, centered, with generous white margin all around. INK is the structure that already existed; RED is the single point of rupture, used on at most 15% of the painted area.
