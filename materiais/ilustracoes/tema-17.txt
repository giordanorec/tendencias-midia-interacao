Minimalist flat geometric illustration in the style of Swiss/Bauhaus diagrams (Josef Müller-Brockmann, Bruno Munari). Square 1024x1024. Pure white background (#FFFFFF), no texture, no shadow, no gradient, no grain. STRICT DUOTONE: exactly two colors of paint — dark ink #231f20 and red #db1e2f — nothing else, no gray, no third color. All lines have one single uniform stroke weight (about 8 px), flat butt ends, ruler-and-compass geometry only (straight lines, circles, arcs, squares, dot grids). Mostly outlines; solid fills only for a few small shapes. The figure occupies about 65% of the square, centered, with generous white margin all around. INK is the structure that already existed; RED is the single point of rupture, used on at most 15% of the painted area. ABSOLUTELY NO text, letters, numbers, logos, icons, robots, brains, circuits, lightbulbs, locks, screens, hands, faces or human figures. No 3D, no perspective, no isometric, no render look. Clean vector look.

SUBJECT (theme 17 — data sovereignty: local-first, end-to-end encryption and passkeys; the data lives on the devices and syncs, the server cannot read what it stores, the third-party identity provider disappears):
In the center, one large hollow INK circle (the server), completely EMPTY inside. Around it, at the corners of the square, four small hollow INK squares (the personal devices), each containing one small SOLID RED dot at its center. Straight INK lines connect the four small squares to each other; the lines pass through the big central circle but the circle stays hollow and empty — nothing red ever touches or enters it. The red dots exist only inside the small squares. Symmetric, calm, diagrammatic. Uniform stroke weight.
